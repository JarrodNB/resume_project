# Ux Friction Loop

Read and follow the [shared protocol](../super-loop/references/iteration-protocol.md) for count selection, isolation, verification, fresh review, publication, and stopping.

Walk an existing flow and identify a reproducible dead end, hidden status, misleading copy, or recovery burden grounded in established product intent or user evidence. Read applicable frontend instructions and the available Impeccable skill. Define the outcome and verify the complete flow before and after, including affected loading, empty, error, success, responsive, and keyboard states. Keep changes focused. Present concrete alternatives and wait for the user's decision when product intent is unresolved; continue only independent work while waiting. Do not invent features to fill quotas.

When coordinated by super-loop, use its selected quota, base, scope, and ledger. Complete one independent iteration at a time without resetting run state or creating a nested quota. Report exhaustion or blockers rather than substituting another category.
